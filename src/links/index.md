  <h1>Links</h1>
  <p>A few neighboring outposts: communities we value and resources for making a creative, independent web. These are places to explore, learn, and find ideas for your own projects.</p>
</header>

<div class="directory-grid">
  <section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="independent-web-heading">
    <h2 id="independent-web-heading">Maker communities</h2>
    <ul class="resource-list">
      <li>
        <strong class="resource-list__name"><a href="https://knoxmakers.org/">Knox Makers</a></strong>
        <span>The maker community and shared workshop in Knoxville where Pablo the Polargraph took shape. A place for exchanging ideas, learning skills, and making things together.</span>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </section>

  <section class="content-section content-section--silver" aria-labelledby="references-heading">
    <h2 id="references-heading">Building an independent web</h2>
    <ul class="resource-list">
      <li>
        <strong class="resource-list__name"><a href="https://indieweb.org/">IndieWeb</a></strong>
        <span>A community built around owning your website, your content, and your online identity. Its guides offer starting points for making a home on the web that belongs to you.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong class="resource-list__name"><a href="https://512kb.club/">512KB Club</a></strong>
        <span>A directory of websites that keep their uncompressed page resources under 512 KB. Its Green Team, under 100 KB, helped shape our own page-weight goal. A place to discover how much personality can fit into a small page.</span>
      </li>
      <li>
        <strong class="resource-list__name"><a href="https://neocities.org/">Neocities</a></strong>
        <span>Static website hosting and a community of people making their own corners of the web. Explore personal sites, art, experiments, and other reminders that a website can be whatever its maker imagines.</span>
      </li>
    </ul>
  </section>
</div>
