<section class="content-section" aria-labelledby="site-heading">
  <h2 id="site-heading">Our mission</h2>
  <p>Starbase 430 is a small outpost for exploring what we can make with the tools, materials, and curiosity we have. Our mission is to follow interesting questions, try unfamiliar techniques, and turn ideas into things we can use, share, or simply enjoy.</p>
  <p>We’re drawn to optimistic futures where technology supports creativity and people share what they learn. Here, that takes shape in electronics, fabric, code, paint, and whatever else a project calls for. Some experiments solve practical problems. Others exist because we wanted to see whether something was possible.</p>
  <p>This site is our record of those explorations. We document the revisions, wrong turns, and discoveries alongside the finished work, with enough detail to give someone else a useful starting point. An unfinished experiment can still have something worth sharing.</p>
</section>
